# Professional email template (not added yet)

Put the professional writing guide here as `guide.md` and the banner as `banner.png` (or `.jpg`), the same way
brands have theirs (brands use `brand_outreach_agent.md` and the three `Step …@2x.png` screenshots at the repo root). You can also paste the guide and upload the banner under
**Setup → Email templates** in the app, which needs no code change or restart.

Until a guide exists, professionals get the built-in marketplace pitch (`backend/app/services/prompts.py`).
Until a banner exists, their emails go out as plain text with no image.
