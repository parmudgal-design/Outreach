Company / network partnership pitch

Used when a contact's type is set to "Company" — diagnostic networks, insurers, health-tech platforms. Proposes that they adopt or integrate Dr.Tracker for their own doctors.

Hospital pitch

Used when a contact's type is set to "Hospital" — a full institutional pilot across departments, pitched to hospital leadership, not a single clinician. Framed around what an administrator tracks: readmissions, discharge follow-up, accreditation, doctor workload.

Product partner pitch

Used when a contact's type is set to "Product partner" — this one runs in the opposite direction. Instead of asking them to adopt Dr.Tracker, it offers Dr.Tracker's own doctor network as a channel for their product (a device, diagnostic, or complementary app doctors could recommend).

Compliance footer

India doesn't have a CAN-SPAM-style law for email specifically, but two things apply here: the IT Act 2000 (no false headers/identity), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, which requires you to give people a clear way to withdraw consent to being contacted and a way to reach you about their data. This footer — sender identity, a contact address, and an opt-out — gets appended automatically to every email; it isn't part of the editable draft, so it can't be deleted by accident.
